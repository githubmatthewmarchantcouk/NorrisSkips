Got questions? No worries!

We get it. Hiring a skip can be daunting, confusing and sometimes downright difficult. Don't worry! We have the answers you're looking for.

Where is the best priced skip hire near me? +
We operate in London and the South East, with sites in Orpington, Bromley and Brixton. And whilst we always try to be competitive and offer a good price, there's more to think about than just the cost. Exceptional customer service, friendly delivery, collection, responsible disposal and recycling of your waste are all factored into our price. We do offer cheap skip hire, but more importantly, we offer exceptional quality from start to finish.
How do I pick the right sized skip? +
Selecting the right skip size can feel daunting. But don't worry, it is actually quite simple. All skips are sized in cubic yards. 1 cubic yard is roughly the size of a washing machine or similar household appliance. So if all your waste (when broken down) would be around the size of 8 washing machines, you'll need an 8 yard skip. Our Skip Selector can help you find the right skip based on your waste type and volume.
How many times will you empty my skip? +
Each skip hire includes one collection and disposal. If you fill the skip up faster than expected or simply underestimated the volume of waste you had, you can arrange an exchange. This means when we come to pick up your skip, we'll leave you an empty one, so there's minimal disruption to your project.
Do you offer skip hire in London? +
Yes. With depots in Greenwich and Brixton, we are able to service residential, commercial and trade projects across London. We offer a full range of solutions, perfectly suited to busy urban environments, from wait and load services where leaving a skip is a challenge, to full commercial solutions for London's biggest developments.
